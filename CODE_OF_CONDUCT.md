# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologising, and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the community and the integrity of the public data.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- sexualised language or imagery and unwanted attention;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission; and
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate GitHub platform violation, use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. For a confidential project report, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/ardjo-s/clean-energy-map/security/advisories/new) and start the title with `Conduct report`. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters. Consequences may range from a private warning to temporary or permanent exclusion, depending on impact, intent, and repeated behaviour.
